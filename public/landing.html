FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need YouTube, TikTok or Instagram developer keys?

No. SlientClip posts through each platform's normal web uploader, the same way you would. You log in once; for YouTube you can use the SlientClip Publisher Chrome extension and post from the Chrome you already use.

Does it keep clipping when my computer is off?

Yes, with server mode. Watching, clipping, captioning and posting all run on the server, so you can close the app and come back to new clips and posts.

How does it decide what's viral?

It reads the stream's chat and compares message rate, emotes, hype words and repeated messages with that channel's normal level. When the spike is big enough for your sensitivity setting, it captures the moment around the peak.

Can I review or edit clips before they're posted?

Always. Edit the title, description, hashtags, caption and subtitles, re-render, post a clip yourself, or remove it from the schedule. Auto-publish only uses clips above the viral score you choose.

Will my YouTube login keep expiring?

Not with the Chrome extension. Uploads run in your own signed-in Chrome, so there's no separate login to keep alive. Logins kept on the server are refreshed automatically, and each account shows a history of why it was kept or lost.

Which languages are subtitles available in?

Subtitles are transcribed automatically in the streamer's broadcast language, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai and more. You can also pick a language yourself.

Is it OK to post clips of other people's streams?

Only post content you have the right or permission to use, and follow each platform's rules. Many streamers welcome clippers, but always check with the creator. SlientClip is a tool; how you use it is up to you.

Can I run it on my own server?

Yes. SlientClip ships as a Docker Compose app with Chromium and ffmpeg included, so you can host your own instance for your team.
